# Design brief

## Story

**Title:** ሕልም የወሰደኝ ጉዞ  
**Story:** A young woman leaves home to attend university, experiences homesickness, learns to manage life independently, and gradually finds friendship and belonging.

## Audience and intent

- **Audience:** University students, especially those living away from home.
- **Intended takeaway:** Leaving home does not mean forgetting it. Growth, resilience, and new friendships can create a second sense of home.
- **Protagonist:** Fictional character, not intended to depict an identifiable real person.
- **Primary language:** Amharic.
- **Delivery formats:** 16:9 horizontal and 9:16 vertical.

The intent is inferred from the closing message of the filmmaker's script and should be confirmed by the filmmaker before picture generation.

## Shot rationale

Each shot's specific purpose is recorded in `storyboard.json`. The sequence moves from departure, through first impressions and homesickness, into independence and friendship, and ends with a broader idea of home.

## Audience tests and revisions

- **Radio cut:** Created at `outputs/radio_cut.mp4` from `outputs/narration_am_v2.wav` (72.46 seconds), with a black picture and a quiet synthesized placeholder tone bed. The filmmaker preferred v2's smoother Leda voice over the earlier Kore take and later Leda takes v3 and v4.
- **Radio-cut audience test:** Completed with three university-student viewers.
  - **Viewer 1:** Liked the cut, but suggested improving the Amharic phrasing around “ወደ አዲስ ራስ” (suggesting “ወደ አዲስ ማንነት” or another more natural expression) and “ከእንግዶች መካከል” (suggesting “ከአዳዲስ ሰዎች መካከል”).
  - **Viewer 2:** Said they liked the short film and described it as very good.
  - **Viewer 3:** Liked everything, and suggested adding other struggles the student might face.
  - **Filmmaker's decision:** Keep the story focused rather than add more struggles, because the runtime is limited. The audience suggested “ወደ አዲስ ማንነት” and “ከአዳዲስ ሰዎች መካከል”, but the filmmaker preferred the smoother delivery of v2. The selected script in `storyboard.json` therefore retains v2's original wording and matches the audio.
